About

I am a second-year Ph.D. student at KAIST, advised by Jinwoo Shin. I received my B.S. degree in Computer Science / Electrical Engineering (double major) at KAIST in 2024. My research interest lies in robotics foundation models for general-purpose robot manipulation. Recently, I am interested in learning robust and transferable representations that enable robots to generalize across diverse tasks, embodiments, and environments.
